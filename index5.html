Biography

Cristina Muccioli is a general practitioner and scholar with a degree from the University of Bologna. Her expertise in telemedicine is complemented by her deep knowledge of ethics and aesthetics, making her a unique voice in healthcare and philosophy. She has built a career fostering wellness through compassionate care and cultural engagement.

Cristina’s work bridges medicine and communication, ensuring patients feel heard and supported, whether in virtual consultations or academic discussions.
